## IMPLEMENTATION

### PATH IDENTITY STRATEGY

- `_canonical_key(path)`: `os.path.normpath(os.path.abspath(...))`, zatim `os.path.realpath`
  kada putanja postoji (resolve symlink/`..`), pa `os.path.normcase` (Windows case-fold;
  no-op na POSIX). Koristi se samo za poređenje identiteta, nikad za textual prefix.
- `_is_within(child, parent)`: `Path.is_relative_to` — structural containment, bez prefix.
- `_find_worktree`: exact canonical match (`_canonical_key(wt.path) == target`).
- `list_flowos_worktrees`: `_is_within(_canonical_key(wt.path), _canonical_key(worktrees_dir))`.
- `_dict_to_info`: `is_main = not _is_within(...)` — glavni i svi unmanaged linked
  worktree-ji su `is_main`.
- fail-closed: unknown/unmanaged/main → `_find_worktree` vraca `None` ili `is_main=True`,
  pa `can_cleanup` vraca `False`.

### PROJECT IDENTITY

- `manager.py` jeste mijenjan (allowed_paths dozvoljava uz dokaz).
- `WorktreeManager.cleanup_worktree()` sada učitava `Project` preko `wt.project_id` i
  vezuje `WorktreeService` za `project.repo_path` (ne `wt.worktree_path`), cime
  `worktrees_dir` i `git worktree list` postaju deterministički. Fail-closed ako project
  nije pronađen.

### FORCE SEMANTICS

- `WorktreeService.cleanup()`: `if not can:` (uklonjen `and not force`). `force=True` i
  dalje dodaje `--force` git flagu, ali vise ne zaobilazi hard identity/scope zaštite
  (unknown/unmanaged/main/konflikt). Dirty/retention policy ostaje u Manageru, nepromenjena.
